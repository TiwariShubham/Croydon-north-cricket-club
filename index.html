About The Club

A Knight's history in the Croydon North community

Croydon North Cricket Club is one of the most successful clubs in the history of the RDCA, fielding junior and senior teams from Under 10s through to our 1st XI. We're a small but proud community club that welcomes new players, families and supporters every season.
