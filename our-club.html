About The Knights

A community cricket club with a proud history in Croydon North.

Our Story

Give… so they may show!!

Croydon North Cricket Club Inc. — known to everyone as the Knights — is one of the most successful clubs in the history of the Ringwood & District Cricket Association (RDCA). For over 100 years the club has been a home ground for cricketers and families across Croydon North.

We're a small but proud community club. Whether you're picking up a bat for the first time in Milo In2Cricket or turning out for the 1st XI, the Knights is about the same thing: a welcoming club, strong club spirit, and giving every player the chance to show what they can do.
